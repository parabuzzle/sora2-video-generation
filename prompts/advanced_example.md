# Advanced Prompt Example

## Overview

This example demonstrates advanced prompting features including camera metadata, lighting controls, dialogue, and input reference images.

## Prompt

A mysterious figure walks through a foggy forest at dusk. The character moves slowly, their silhouette barely visible through the mist. As they approach, their face becomes clearer, revealing a contemplative expression.

## Video Settings

| model | sora-2-pro |
| duration | 12 seconds |
| orientation | landscape |

## Camera (optional)

Wide shot transitioning to medium closeup. 85mm lens. Shallow depth of field (f/2.8). Slow dolly-in movement. Camera starts at 10 feet distance, ends at 4 feet.

## Lighting (optional)

Golden hour lighting, soft and diffused. Color palette: deep forest green, amber, warm grey. Volumetric lighting through fog. Rim lighting on character's silhouette.

## Dialogue (optional)

"The path forward isn't always clear, but we walk it nonetheless."
